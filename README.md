## This is me

Hi, I'm **Pau**, an Embedded Software Engineer and Product Owner at Technica Electronics in Barcelona.
I write firmware that sits right at the hardware boundary, and I use AI agents to make that work faster without making it sloppier.

- 🔌 **C/C++ firmware for ARM Cortex-M**: drivers, RTOS applications, Ethernet, CAN, UART, SPI and I2C, debugged on target with Lauterbach TRACE32.
- 🚗 Previously at **Technica Engineering in Munich**, building automotive Ethernet test tools in an international team.
- 🧭 **Product owner** for several products, from requirements to release, and the technical contact for customers and suppliers.
- 🤖 Deep in **Claude Code**: skills, hooks and MCP servers for embedded and tooling work.
- 🦾 Learning in public: **ROS 2 Jazzy**, micro-ROS and embedded Linux.
- 🎓 M.Sc. Electronic Engineering at UPC Barcelona, with an Erasmus year at TUM Munich.
- 🌍 Working in Catalan, Spanish, English and German (B1).
- 💬 Talk to me about **firmware that talks to hardware you can't see** or **AI agents for embedded engineering**.
